Privacy Policy

What ShipLink collects, why, and what you can remove.

Last updated 2026-08-29 · Operated by EagLeoX LLC

What we collect

Your name and email address, to identify your account. Your city and country, and — if you are a service provider — the postal code your services depart from. Your device location, only while you are using the app and only if you allow it. Photos you choose to upload, for your profile picture and for packages. Identity documents, only if you apply to become a service provider. The messages you send to other members. Your shipments, requests and the reviews you exchange.

Why we collect it

To run the marketplace: to show you providers travelling near you, to let you and a provider agree a shipment, to let you message each other, and to build the trust signals — verification status and completed shipment counts — that help members decide who to ship with. We do not use any of it for advertising.

Your location

Location is requested only when you open the map, and only for use while the app is open. We never collect it in the background. If you decline, the app still works — you see every provider, but without distances.

What other members can see

Your display name, initials, profile picture, city and country, and your trust signals. If you publish a service, other members see the cities you travel between, your dates, your price and your capacity.

They never see your exact location. A provider’s departure point is rounded to roughly one kilometre before it is published, and the precise coordinates, street address and postal code are readable only by that provider. Your email address, phone number and identity documents are never shown to other members. A phone number is shared with the other party only once a shipment request has been accepted.

Who else has access

ShipLink uses Supabase to store data and files, Google Maps for maps and address lookup on Android devices, and Expo to build and deliver the app. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Tracking

ShipLink does not track you. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracker in the app, and we do not link your activity to data from other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account is open.

When you close your account, you can no longer sign in. Your name, profile picture, location, phone number and identity documents are removed. Completed shipments, their tracking history, the reviews you exchanged and your messages stay on record for the other members involved, detached from your identity. The email address used to create the account remains in the sign-in system, but it can no longer be used to sign in.

We keep the shared record because deleting your side would erase the other member’s shipment history and the reviews they wrote. One person’s right to erasure is not a right to delete another person’s records.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time from the Profile tab. You can close your account from the same screen — the app tells you exactly what is deleted before you confirm. You can turn location off in iOS Settings without losing access to the app. If you have an active shipment, you will be asked to complete or cancel it before closing your account, because it is a commitment to someone else.

Children

ShipLink is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For any question about your data, or to request a copy of it, contact us at the support address in the Profile tab. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Also available inside the ShipLink app, under Profile → Legal & Privacy.
